How it works

How the fee loop works.

Pons prices the curve in ETH. The one thing that changes is the creator-fee recipient: an adapter contract.

01

Launch on Pons

Token created with an ETH pair, fees pointed at the adapter.

02

Fees accrue

Trading fees sweep into the escrow ledger the adapter holds.

03

Claim & buy

Owner triggers the claim; ETH swaps to RBLX with min-out.

04

Into treasury

RBLX lands in the treasury. Adapter holds no balance after.
